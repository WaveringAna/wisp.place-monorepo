Our Principles

How We Collect Information

Signing in

wisp.place uses the AT Protocol for authentication. When you log in, you authenticate directly with your Personal Data Server (PDS): the wisp.place dashboard never sees your password. What it receives and stores is your Decentralized Identifier (DID), a public pseudonymous identifier that is the foundation of your AT Protocol identity. The dashboard does not collect email addresses or phone numbers. If your account lives on pds.wisp.place, that server does hold your email address; see accounts on pds.wisp.place.

During the login process, your AT Protocol handle (e.g. you.bsky.social) may appear in application logs. It is not stored in the database.

Your sites

When you publish a site, we store metadata about it: the site name, display name, your wisp.place subdomain, any custom domains you configure, content hashes, timestamps, and your site settings. The actual files you upload live on your PDS as blobs; we hold a derived cache to serve them to visitors.

Logs and statistics

We retain server logs and collect non-personally-identifying operational data (request paths, HTTP methods, status codes, and response durations) to monitor service health and diagnose issues. The wisp.place app and hosting servers do not log IP addresses, User-Agent strings, or Referer headers. The one exception is sign-in devices on pds.wisp.place, described below.

Request counts are aggregated into periodic hit totals per site. These aggregate statistics contain no personally identifying information (no IP addresses, no geographic data, no visitor identifiers of any kind) and are made available to site owners who support the project.

Cookies

We use a signed session cookie to keep you logged into your account. Session records are stored in the database and expire after 30 days.

Accounts on pds.wisp.place

If you sign up through wisp.place, your AT Protocol account is created on pds.wisp.place, a PDS we operate (running Tranquil PDS). Signing in with an account hosted elsewhere does not involve this server. For accounts it hosts, it stores:

  • •Your email address, and whether you have verified it
  • •Your handle, DID, and a hash of your password (never the password itself), plus any app passwords, passkeys, or two-factor settings you add
  • •If you sign up or sign in with GitHub: your GitHub user ID, username, and the email address GitHub provides
  • •For each device you sign in from: its IP address and User-Agent, and when it was last seen, so you can review your sessions and so suspicious sign-ins can be spotted
  • •Your repository: every record and blob you publish, including your sites and anything other AT Protocol apps write to your account
  • •Emails queued to you (verification codes, password resets, security notices) until they are sent

We use your email address only to run your account: verifying it, resetting your password, sending sign-in and security codes, and contacting you about your account or the service. It is never used for marketing, never sold, and never shared except to deliver those emails.

Your site content is fetched from your AT Protocol PDS. Unless that is pds.wisp.place, we do not control the data practices of your PDS provider.

How We Protect Your Information

Session cookies are httpOnly, Secure, and use SameSite=Lax. OAuth authorization states expire after one hour. Signing keys are rotated every six months.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Data Retention

  • SessionsDashboard session records expire automatically after 30 days.
  • Sites and domainsKept until you delete your site. When you delete your place.wisp.fs record from your PDS, the hosting service receives that event via the AT Protocol firehose and automatically removes the corresponding cache and database entries. For complete removal of your data from our systems, contact privacy@wisp.place in addition to deleting your PDS records.
  • Logs and metricsIn-memory log buffers, error traces, and metrics are rolling windows and are not persisted beyond the monitoring stack.
  • pds.wisp.place accountKept, with its email address, until you delete it from the account settings on pds.wisp.place or ask at privacy@wisp.place. Deleting it removes your email address, credentials, linked GitHub identity, sign-in devices, and repository from the server.
  • Sign-in devicesDevice records (IP address, User-Agent, last activity) are deleted with your account, and automatically after 90 days without use. A device still trusted to skip two-factor codes is kept until that trust expires, and a device another pds.wisp.place account also signs in on stays for that account.
  • Beyond our serversTwo things outlive deletion by design of the protocol: records already copied by other AT Protocol services, and your DID's history in the public PLC directory (which holds your handle and PDS address, never your email).

The AT Protocol and Public Data

wisp.place is built on the AT Protocol. Your sites, their files, and the records describing them are public on your PDS. Anyone operating a compatible service can read your published site records and blobs. This is inherent to how the protocol works and is not specific to wisp.place. On pds.wisp.place the same holds for every record in your repository; your email address, password, and devices are private and never published.

The CLI Tool

The wisp.place CLI operates entirely on your local machine. It communicates directly with your PDS and sends no usage data or telemetry to us. OAuth sessions are stored locally in a directory of your choice.
