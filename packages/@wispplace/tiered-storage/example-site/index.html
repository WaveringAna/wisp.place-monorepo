Features

⚡

Hot Tier (Memory)

Lightning-fast access with LRU eviction. Perfect for frequently accessed data like index.html.

💾

Warm Tier (Disk)

Fast local storage with configurable eviction policies. Ideal for site assets and media.

☁️

Cold Tier (S3)

Unlimited cloud storage as your source of truth. Supports S3, R2, MinIO, and more.
